1. Document Control & Purpose
2. Purpose, Scope & Objectives
The POS engine is the controlled point at which a product selection becomes a legally and financially meaningful sale. It must calculate the correct price, tax, weight, batch allocation, discount, payment, customer credit impact, inventory movement, accounting entries and receipt while remaining safe during offline operation, device failure, concurrent sales and delayed external payments.
Support retail, farmer, wholesale and Aavin sub-contractor transactions.
Support sealed bags and loose/fractional feed.
Support barcode, Tamil Unicode, transliteration, colloquial and phonetic product discovery.
Integrate digital weighing scales without allowing unstable readings to silently become sale quantities.
Allocate sale quantities against eligible batches using configured FIFO/FEFO policy.
Support cash, UPI, bank, Khata and split tender.
Create immutable finalized invoice snapshots for price, tax and product/UOM context.
Provide printer and WhatsApp receipt fallbacks without coupling them to financial finalization.
Support offline POS with SQLCipher and deterministic reconciliation.
Provide traceability from invoice → line → batch → stock movement → payment → journal → audit.
3. Non-Goals
POS is not the authoritative inventory ledger; the inventory engine remains the inventory authority.
POS does not independently invent GST rates, HSN/SAC or legal thresholds.
POS does not mark UPI as paid based solely on Flutter/client state.
Finalized invoices are not edited in place.
Post-finalization corrections are handled through returns, credit notes or authorized reversals.
Long-running external integrations must not hold the core invoice database transaction open.
4. Architecture & Principles
Flutter owns presentation, local cart state, device integration and offline transaction capture.
Go owns business rules, validation, authorization, transaction orchestration and external integration boundaries.
PostgreSQL is authoritative for finalized financial/inventory state.
RLS and application authorization both enforce tenant isolation.
All money uses fixed-precision decimal/Numeric; never binary floating-point for financial calculations.
All finalized financial writes are atomic and idempotent.
External side effects use outbox/event processing where appropriate.
Printer, WhatsApp, soundbox and WebSocket failures never roll back a valid finalized invoice.
Financial and inventory conflicts are explicitly reconciled; last-write-wins is prohibited.
Finalized invoice values are snapshots; future master-data changes do not rewrite historical invoices.
5. POS Sale Lifecycle
Open/select POS session and device.
Create or resume draft cart.
Identify customer or use Walk-In customer.
Search/select product and UOM.
Capture quantity or scale weight.
Resolve effective price and tax profile.
Apply configured customer tier and authorized discounts.
Validate stock and reserve where the architecture requires reservation.
Calculate line totals, tax and invoice totals.
Select payment method(s).
Create payment intent for asynchronous payment methods where required.
Confirm all payment requirements are satisfied or transaction is explicitly allowed as Khata/pending according to policy.
Finalize the invoice in one server transaction.
Post stock movement, accounting/Khata, payment allocations and outbox event.
Generate receipt representation.
Print asynchronously/locally; offer WhatsApp fallback if printer fails.
Mark cart completed and retain immutable audit trail.

7. Product Search & Discovery
7.1 Search inputs
Exact barcode and alternate barcodes.
Product name in Tamil Unicode.
English name.
Tamil transliteration/phonetic spelling.
Colloquial farmer terms and regional abbreviations.
Configured aliases and historical approved aliases.
Normalized search removing harmless punctuation/spacing variations.
Fuzzy search only when exact/prefix results are insufficient.
7.2 Search ranking
Exact barcode.
Exact canonical product code.
Exact canonical name.
Exact approved alias.
Prefix canonical/alias.
Tokenized Tamil/English match.
Configured transliteration/phonetic match.
Fuzzy match.
Fuzzy results must be visibly distinguished and must not silently select a product. The cashier must confirm the result when ambiguity exists.
7.3 Unknown barcode
Show an explicit unknown-barcode state.
Allow authorized search by name/code.
Never auto-create a product from a scan.
Offer product registration only to permitted roles and outside the finalized sale flow.
8. Customer Selection & Pricing Tiers
These defaults are starting configuration values, not immutable code. Effective-dated price lists determine the price at sale time.
Customer tier changes require authorization and audit.
Customer-specific negotiated prices may override tier price only when configured.
Price selection records price_list_id, price_list_item_id/version/effective period, base price, discount rule and final unit price.
Finalized invoice stores the resolved price/discount snapshot.
9. Price Resolution
Validate product is saleable and not archived/blocked.
Resolve requested UOM and product-UOM relationship.
Find active effective-dated price list for tenant/customer/tier/channel.
Apply product/customer/UOM-specific price rule.
Apply configured tier discount if applicable.
Apply authorized manual discount if allowed.
Validate minimum margin if the tenant enables margin protection.
Round only at defined calculation boundaries.
Persist the resolved price snapshot at finalization.
A stale local price must not silently overwrite a newer server price. Offline policy determines whether a sale may proceed using the last approved local price and whether reconciliation requires exception review.
10. UOM, Quantity & Loose Feed
Sealed bag sales use the configured product/UOM quantity.
Loose sales use kg or configured loose UOM.
Bag-to-loose conversion is permitted only when product-UOM conversion exists.
Conversion must not create stock from nothing; every decrement must correspond to available inventory.
Display can show sealed bags plus loose kg where useful, but the authoritative ledger records normalized quantities and UOM context.
11. Digital Scale Integration
11.1 Reading lifecycle
Connect to paired scale.
Receive raw reading.
Parse protocol and unit.
Validate numeric range.
Convert to configured sale UOM.
Apply stabilization rule.
Display live weight.
Require stable reading before automatic commit.
Record capture source, device ID, timestamp and raw/normalized value.
11.2 Stabilization
Tenant/device configuration defines minimum stability duration and allowed variance.
An unstable reading is displayed as unstable and cannot be accepted automatically.
Manual entry is available only when policy permits and must be audited.
Scale disconnect during draft sale does not destroy the cart.
Scale reading must be revalidated at finalization.
11.3 Manual weight
Require permission such as POS_WEIGHT_MANUAL.
Require reason.
Record operator, device and timestamp.
Do not bypass product-UOM precision or stock validation.
12. Batch Allocation: FIFO/FEFO
Only eligible batches are allocatable.
Expired, held, rejected, recalled or blocked batches are excluded.
Default policy is FEFO when expiry exists and FIFO as the secondary ordering rule; tenant/product configuration may define policy.
Allocation must be deterministic.
Invoice lines store batch allocation records.
Stock movements reference the selected batch.
Where batch is not meaningful for a product, allocation may use a non-batch inventory policy.
Eligible batches:
available_qty > 0
AND status = ACTIVE
AND quality_status = ACCEPTED
AND NOT expired
AND NOT held/recalled

Order:
FEFO expiry_date ASC NULLS LAST
then received_at ASC
then batch_id ASC
The inventory engine remains responsible for authoritative allocation and locking. POS requests allocation; it must not independently decrement stock.
13. Stock Availability & Concurrency
At cart time, show availability as an advisory value unless a reservation is explicitly created.
At finalization, inventory is revalidated under transaction/locking rules.
Concurrent sales cannot cause negative stock unless an explicit tenant policy allows controlled negative stock.
Stale offline stock can result in reconciliation failure/exception.
Allocation failures return machine-readable business errors.
Stock reservation, if enabled, has expiration and release behavior.
Recommended locking sequence: invoice/payment validation → product/UOM checks → inventory allocation/row locks in deterministic product/batch order → stock movements → accounting/ledger → invoice finalization.
14. Cart Calculation Engine
The calculation engine must be deterministic and independently unit-testable.
For each line:
gross = quantity × resolved_unit_price
line_discount = configured/manual discount
taxable_value = gross - line_discount + taxable_adjustments
tax = tax_engine(taxable_value, tax_profile, place_of_supply)
line_total = taxable_value + tax

Invoice:
subtotal = sum(line gross)
discount_total = sum(line discounts) + invoice-level discount
taxable_total = sum(line taxable values)
tax_total = sum(line taxes)
rounding_adjustment = configured rounding policy
grand_total = taxable_total + tax_total + rounding_adjustment
Never use float arithmetic.
Use one canonical rounding strategy defined by finance configuration.
Store calculation inputs and outputs required for audit.
Do not repeatedly round intermediate values unless the configured accounting rule requires it.
Finalized invoice totals must reconcile to line/tax/payment totals.
15. Discounts & Authorization
Every manual discount records actor, reason, original value, final value and authorization.
Discount cannot produce negative line price.
Discount cannot exceed configured maximum unless owner override is recorded.
Tax calculation uses the post-discount taxable base according to configured tax treatment.
16. GST & Tax Engine
Tax rates and tax profiles are configuration-driven.
Support tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing.
Support CGST + SGST for applicable intra-state transactions and IGST for applicable inter-state transactions.
Store HSN/tax code on product/tax configuration where required.
Determine place-of-supply/tax treatment from tenant/customer transaction configuration.
Tax-exempt/zero-rated treatment must be explicit and auditable.
Do not hardcode a universal 0% or 18% rule; examples are configuration data.
Finalized invoice stores tax profile/version/rate/split and calculated amounts.
Tax master changes affect new transactions only unless an explicit re-rating process is authorized.
Tax rounding must be deterministic at line or invoice level according to the configured accounting policy, and the policy must be consistent with the accounting engine and GST export.
17. Invoice Numbering
Document series is tenant + financial year + document type scoped.
Server-authoritative finalized invoice number must be unique.
Offline devices may use provisional/local IDs and later receive authoritative numbering.
Do not use client-generated final invoice numbers as the sole authority.
Sequence allocation must be concurrency-safe.
Number collisions are never resolved by overwriting an existing invoice.
Voided/unused numbers follow the tenant's controlled document-series policy and audit requirements.
Finalized invoice stores financial_year_id and document_series_id.
18. Multi-Tender & Payments
The sum of accepted payment allocations must exactly reconcile with the invoice amount within the configured minor-unit/rounding rule.
18.1 Payment intent
Create intent with unique idempotency key.
Persist expected amount and invoice/cart reference.
Generate dynamic QR when supported.
Wait for verified webhook or trusted status API.
Apply signature verification and idempotency.
Update intent state.
Allocate payment only after authoritative paid state.
Handle timeout/expiry without treating it as paid.
18.2 WebSocket and polling
WebSocket provides low-latency UI updates only.
Webhook/status API remains authoritative.
If WebSocket fails, POS polls status with bounded backoff.
Client never sets paid=true as an authority.
Duplicate webhooks are harmless through event idempotency.
Late success after UI timeout must reconcile to the same intent, not create a duplicate payment.
19. Payment State Machine
A sale must not be finalized as a cash/UPI-paid invoice merely because the client UI reports success. The finalization service verifies the payment state.
20. Khata / Credit Sale
Resolve customer credit profile.
Calculate current outstanding exposure including pending/posted relevant entries.
Calculate available credit = limit − exposure, using the accounting engine's authoritative method.
Validate requested Khata allocation.
Reject if over limit unless the role has override permission.
Check overdue/grace policy where configured.
Owner/admin PIN override is a controlled authorization event, not a bypass.
Finalize invoice and receivable/Khata ledger atomically.
Expose new outstanding balance in the result.
Credit limit and customer status can change between cart and finalization; final validation occurs server-side.
Override records actor, approver, reason, timestamp and device.
Collection grace/Aavin cycle rules are configuration-driven and do not change invoice mathematics.
21. Atomic Invoice Finalization
Finalization is the central POS transaction boundary.
Authenticate device/user and authorize operation.
Validate tenant, financial year and document series.
Validate cart state and idempotency key.
Revalidate product/UOM/tax/price snapshots and saleability according to online/offline policy.
Revalidate customer/credit state.
Verify payment states or create authorized receivable.
Allocate/lock inventory through the inventory engine.
Calculate final totals using canonical decimal rules.
Insert sales invoice header.
Insert invoice lines.
Insert invoice tax lines.
Insert batch allocation rows.
Insert stock movements through inventory service.
Insert payment allocations/receivable entries.
Post accounting journal entries.
Create audit entries.
Write outbox/integration events.
Commit once.
Only after commit trigger print/share/notifications asynchronously or locally.
No partial invoice is considered finalized. If any mandatory component fails, the database transaction rolls back.
22. Idempotency
Every finalize request carries an Idempotency-Key.
Key is scoped to tenant + operation/device as defined by API policy.
Same key + same request fingerprint returns the original result.
Same key + different payload returns an idempotency conflict.
Webhook event IDs are unique and idempotent.
Retry after timeout must not create a second invoice, stock movement or payment allocation.
Offline replay uses the local transaction envelope ID as a stable idempotency identity.
23. Offline POS
23.1 Supported offline capabilities
Browse cached products and aliases.
Create cart and calculate using cached approved price/tax snapshots.
Capture barcode and scale readings.
Record cash sales subject to offline policy.
Record permitted Khata sales subject to cached credit policy plus server reconciliation.
Create provisional invoice/local transaction envelope.
Queue synchronization when connectivity returns.
Print locally if the device has a paired printer.
23.2 Restricted offline operations
UPI should not be treated as settled solely offline.
Fresh server credit-limit validation is unavailable offline.
High-risk discounts may be disabled.
Compliance actions requiring current external verification may be disabled.
Stock allocation is provisional and must reconcile against server inventory.
23.3 Recovery
Persist cart and transaction envelope atomically in SQLCipher.
On app restart, identify OPEN/QUEUED/UPLOADING/UNKNOWN envelopes.
Resume upload or reconcile status.
Never create a new invoice merely because the previous request timed out.
Resolve conflicts explicitly.
24. Receipt Rendering & Printing
Receipt data is generated from finalized invoice snapshot, not current product master.
Support ESC/POS thermal printing.
Support Tamil text where printer/font capability allows.
Provide a fallback representation for printers that cannot render Tamil, such as configured transliteration/alternate text.
Reprint requires permission and creates audit event.
Print failure never rolls back financial finalization.
Receipt should include invoice number/date, shop identity, GST information where applicable, customer, line details, quantity/UOM, rate, discount, tax breakup, total, tender summary, balance/change and configured footer.
24.1 WhatsApp fallback
After printer failure, offer one-tap receipt sharing.
WhatsApp delivery is asynchronous.
Failure to send WhatsApp does not alter invoice/payment state.
Consent, phone validation and template governance apply.
Delivery attempts are logged separately.
25. Returns, Refunds & Credit Notes
Locate original finalized invoice.
Validate returnable quantity and item eligibility.
Use original invoice/batch traceability where required.
Determine return condition and restock/damage disposition.
Create sales return and return lines.
Create inventory return movement if restockable.
Create refund or customer credit allocation according to chosen settlement.
Create credit note/financial reversal records where applicable.
Reverse relevant tax/accounting impacts through controlled journal/reversal logic.
Audit all relationships back to the original invoice.
Return quantity cannot exceed remaining returnable quantity.
Refund cannot exceed refundable amount.
Batch traceability is retained even if stock is returned to a different location.
Returns do not mutate the original invoice lines.
26. Cancellations, Voids & Reversals
Draft/held carts may be cancelled without financial impact.
Finalized invoices cannot be deleted.
Post-finalization correction uses return, credit note, refund or authorized reversal.
Void behavior for document numbering is controlled by financial-year/document-series policy.
Every reversal references the original transaction and records reason/actor.
27. Hold / Resume Cart
Hold stores a complete cart snapshot and operator/device context.
Hold does not permanently reserve stock unless reservation is explicitly enabled.
Price/tax/stock are revalidated on resume and again at finalization.
Expired holds cannot be silently finalized.
Customer information and notes are retained with the hold.
28. POS Session, Cash Drawer & EOD
POS session may be opened with opening cash float.
Cash sales and payouts are linked to the active cash session.
Drawer operations are audited.
Expected cash is derived from accounting/cash movement records.
EOD compares expected cash with physical count and records variance.
Printer/payment hardware failure does not invalidate the cash session.
EOD cannot silently modify historical sales.
Expected cash =
opening cash
+ cash sales
+ other authorized cash-in
- cash refunds
- cash payouts
- cash withdrawals
± other configured cash movements

Optional customer-facing display may show cart lines, subtotal, tax and total.
During UPI payment, it may display amount and QR.
Customer display is informational and never authoritative.

39. Critical Edge Cases
Duplicate barcode scan: add quantity only according to configured scan behavior; avoid accidental double-sale where confirmation is required.
Unknown barcode: do not auto-create product.
Stale price: server revalidation wins online; offline policy determines acceptance/reconciliation.
Stale stock: final inventory allocation decides; no client-only decrement.
Batch expires during cart: finalization excludes it and reallocates or rejects.
Scale disconnect: preserve cart; manual entry only when permitted.
Printer disconnect: invoice remains finalized; retry/fallback.
UPI delayed: intent remains pending; later verified event reconciles without duplicate invoice/payment.
Payment duplicate: event idempotency prevents duplicate allocation.
Split-tender rounding: canonical minor-unit reconciliation; no unexplained residual.
Overpayment: reject or place excess into explicitly supported customer credit/refund workflow; never silently discard.
Credit limit changes: finalization rechecks current authority online.
Device crash: recover cart/envelope and safely replay.
App restart: no duplicate finalize.
Concurrent sale: inventory locking prevents double allocation.
Offline conflict: explicit reconciliation; no LWW.
Invoice number collision: transaction fails safely; no overwrite.
Tax profile change: historical invoice remains unchanged; new sale uses current effective profile.
Product archived: historical invoices remain readable; new sale rejected.
Printer fails after finalization: financial transaction remains successful.
Payment webhook arrives before UI returns: backend records it; subsequent status check finds same intent.
Return after batch transfer: trace original batch and current location/disposition.
Discount causes negative total: reject.
Zero/negative weight: reject unless a specifically configured non-weighted product flow applies.
Customer phone missing: WhatsApp option disabled or asks for valid consented number.
Clock skew: server timestamps authoritative for finalization; client time retained only as device metadata.

44. Security Requirements
All authoritative APIs use HTTPS.
WebSocket uses authenticated WSS.
Tenant context is derived from authenticated session/device context, not trusted from arbitrary request body.
PostgreSQL RLS is mandatory.
Sensitive PIN/approval verification is server-side.
Idempotency records cannot be tenant-crossed.
Payment webhook signatures must be verified.
Device credentials can be revoked.
SQLCipher database keys are stored using platform secure storage.
Rooted/jailbroken device policy follows Document 04.
Rate limiting applies to authentication, payment status, finalize and other abuse-prone endpoints.
Audit logs are append-oriented and protected from ordinary cashier mutation.
45. Data Integrity Invariants
Finalized invoice total equals its persisted calculation result.
Invoice tax lines reconcile with invoice tax total.
Payment allocations never exceed permitted amount without explicit overpayment workflow.
Stock movements reconcile to invoice batch allocations.
Returnable quantity never becomes negative.
Accounting journal is balanced.
Khata ledger and invoice receivable reconcile.
Every finalized invoice has unique authoritative number.
Every authoritative financial command is idempotent.
No finalized invoice is deleted.
Historical invoice snapshots remain stable after master-data changes.
Tenant-owned records cannot be read or written across tenant boundaries.

48. Acceptance Criteria
A cashier can complete a normal cash sale end-to-end without manual database intervention.
A loose-feed sale captures a stable scale weight and correctly decrements inventory.
Customer pricing tiers resolve according to effective configuration.
GST calculation is configuration-driven and auditable.
FIFO/FEFO allocation excludes expired/held/rejected batches.
Concurrent finalization cannot oversell inventory.
Cash, UPI, bank and Khata tenders reconcile exactly.
Split tender cannot finalize with an unexplained balance.
UPI is never accepted based only on client state.
Khata credit limits are enforced and overrides are audited.
Invoice finalization is atomic across invoice, tax, stock, payment/ledger, accounting and outbox.
Retrying the same finalize command cannot duplicate the sale.
Offline transactions survive app restart and reconcile safely.
Printer failure cannot undo a finalized invoice.
WhatsApp failure cannot undo a finalized invoice.
Returns cannot exceed remaining returnable quantities.
Finalized invoices are immutable.
RLS prevents cross-tenant POS access.
All critical operations have audit evidence.
All end-to-end scenarios in Section 43 pass.
All non-negotiable rules below are enforced by automated tests.
49. Non-Negotiable POS Rules
Never use floating-point arithmetic for money or precision-sensitive weight calculations.
Never trust a client-only paid flag.
Never finalize a sale without authoritative or explicitly permitted offline validation.
Never decrement stock outside the inventory transaction boundary.
Never edit a finalized invoice in place.
Never overwrite an existing invoice because of a numbering collision.
Never use last-write-wins for financial/inventory conflicts.
Never allow expired/held/rejected batches into allocation.
Never silently create a product from an unknown barcode.
Never let printer/WhatsApp failure roll back a finalized financial transaction.
Never duplicate payment or invoice outcomes on retries.
Never bypass RLS or application tenant authorization.
Never hardcode GST rates, legal thresholds or customer discount percentages that are intended to be configuration.
Never hide manual weight, discount, credit override or reversal activity from audit.
Never treat offline provisional state as automatically authoritative without reconciliation.
